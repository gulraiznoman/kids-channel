When giving YouTube/FB meta, the title must start with the highest-demand search phrase that truthfully fits the video. Don't use story wording (e.g. "Too Much Candy! Tummy Ache Story") just because autocomplete lists it.

**Why:** 2026-10-25 he rejected the S19 title: "people don't search with these keywords". The demand check showed "too much candy" was weak, while "No No Snacks" (449M top video), "Healthy Habits for Kids" (244M) and "Eat Healthy Food" (133M) are strong.

**How to apply:**
1. Pull autocomplete from broad seeds (suggestqueries ds=yt).
2. Rank the candidate phrases by top/median views of their search results (the scratchpad demand.py approach).
3. Put the 2-3 strongest accurate phrases in the title, the strongest first, and the story hook after it.
4. Never use channel names. Related: [[feedback-kids-no-meta]], [[project-youtube-kids]].
