For Gul & Mishu videos, draw animals as **full-body cartoon animals** (sitting/standing, legs, paws, tail,
collar on the puppy, big shiny eyes), like typical cute cartoon puppy illustrations. Head-only round faces
read as "avatars" to him. He also did NOT want real stock video clips when I proposed them: he meant
cartoon, just with real animal shapes.

**Why:** toddlers must recognise the animal from its real body shape; floating heads don't map to real
animals in their memory.

**How to apply:** use `kids-channel/long01-animals-fun/animals_v2.py` style for every animal; put a soft
white "spotlight" circle behind each item so it reads on busy backgrounds. Real animal SOUNDS from Mixkit
(sfxFree) are fine and kept; check titles (no "rubber duck", "cinematic riser"). See [[project-youtube-kids]].
