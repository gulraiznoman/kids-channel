He called the plain thumbnail (flat colour + one object + 2 words, from `tools/thumb_simple.py`) "boring" on 2026-10-20:
it must be "very appealing for mothers and kids to click".

What he accepted (L04 THUMBNAIL v2, `long04-good-habits/thumb2.py`): bright two-colour sunburst with a warm glow,
Gul & Mishu big with open-mouth happy faces DOING the video's activity (brushing with foam, washing hands with bubbles),
a cute mascot in the middle (smiling sparkly tooth with cheeks), stars and bubbles, big rainbow title on top
(still short, 2 words). Keep effects off the faces.

**Why:** parents decide from the thumbnail; it has to show fun and action at a glance.
**How to apply:** use `kids-channel/tools/thumb_fun.py` (sunburst, kids hold props in their raised hands, Mishu mirrored so her prop faces centre, mascot/centre piece, rainbow title); add a function per video. Save as a new version, never overwrite. Done: L04 v3, L02 v2, L03 v2.
