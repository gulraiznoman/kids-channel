Standing rules for every Gul & Mishu upload (he asked to keep them "from now onwards", 2026-10-18):

1. **Made for Kids stays ON** for every video and Short.
2. **Don't look mass-produced**: vary format, length and style between videos (story, game, song/chant, learning, countdown...), never one template on repeat. YouTube refuses monetisation for near-identical templated channels.
3. **Max one upload per day** (a Short OR a long video, not both). On long-video days, skip that day's Short.
4. **Audio: only sounds/music we make ourselves (synthesised in code, Kokoro voices) or from YouTube's Audio Library.** No Mixkit or other stock sites from now on (the kitten meow in H01 and the L01 animal sounds came from Mixkit sfxFree).
5. **Accurate titles, thumbnails, hashtags**: nothing trending or unrelated just for reach, no other channels' names.
7. **No repetitive videos** (he stressed this 2026-10-18): before building ANY video, read `kids-channel/CONTENT LOG.md`
   and check topic+format, items (max ~1/3 reused, shown a new way), script, look. Add each new video to the log.
6. **Audience is kids about 2-7, not only toddlers**: say "for Kids" in titles, and give each long video something for older kids (e.g. adding, harder questions).

**Why:** he wants the channel monetisable and safe from penalties; kids channels get hit for templated mass content and misclassified audience.
**How to apply:** check all six before delivering any video, metadata or schedule. See [[project-youtube-kids]].
