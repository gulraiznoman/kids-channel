He rejected Body Parts v1 (2026-10-21): "very bad, not much informative, music too boring, starting should be very
impressive, why ABC written behind; look for other videos on YouTube on this topic".

Rules from that:
1. **Research first**: before scripting a topic, scrape YouTube search for the topic (scratchpad `yt_topic.py`) and read the
   top videos' titles/descriptions/chapters. Copy their DEPTH (e.g. 25-30 body parts + "inside the human body"), not their content.
2. **Informative**: many items with a real fact each ("My eyelashes keep dust out of my eyes"), plus an older-kids layer.
3. **Music must be exciting**: dance/pop beat ~124 BPM with kick, snare/clap, hats, bouncy bass, catchy lead
   (`long05-body-parts/make2.py` dance_music_mix). Soft marimba/ukulele loops are "boring".
4. **Big opening in the first 3-7 s**: drum roll, crash + confetti, logo spin, characters jump in, title slam with flash, then a funny hook.
5. **Backgrounds must match the topic**: no unrelated decorations (the ABC border on a body video confused him).

**Why:** retention data shows kids leave at ~1.5 min; he wants videos that feel as good as big kids channels.
**How to apply:** every new long video. Related: [[feedback-kids-channel-safety]], [[feedback-kids-thumbnails]], [[project-youtube-kids]].
