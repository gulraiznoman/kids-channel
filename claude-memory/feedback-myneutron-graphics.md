**Captions:** short. Three lines, roughly 110 to 200 characters. One caption used unchanged on both X and LinkedIn, not separate versions. Most posts want a CTA with the URL, worded differently each time, not `myneutron.ai` pasted on everything.

**Brand is atomic, not agricultural.** The visual language is a glowing nucleus with orbital rings and pearl-like particles. "Seeds" is the name of the unit, not a gardening metaphor. A seed-packet brief was rejected on that basis.

**Graphics:** 16:9, one asset per post used on both channels, title top-centre, logo bottom-right, generous clear margin on all four sides, same background colour to every edge. Short, engaging headings, roughly three or four words.

**What gets called "dead":** flat even lighting, a plain cream background, small static objects floating in a void, neat tidy props. Their strongest asset (the Storage vs Memory article graphic) works because it has a dark-to-bright gradient, real chaos on one side, particles in transit, and the atom glowing as hero on the other. Every brief needs a light gradient and something caught mid-action rather than a finished state.

**Why:** the plan they inherited had every brief as the same abstract glowing orb, so the feed looked identical post to post. Vary the visual medium deliberately and list it in the sheet.

**How to apply:** write briefs concrete to the product the way [[feedback-inflectiv-copy]] demands, pair each brief with a short Nano Banana prompt plus a negative prompt, and note that the logo mark must be composited into the nucleus afterwards because the image model will not draw it.
