For every YouTube deliverable he asks for:

- Always supply a search-optimised title, a description with hashtags, and tags,
  copy-paste ready in the reply itself, not only in a file.
- Never put the duration ("4 minutes") in the title or on the thumbnail.
- One folder per video under `ambient-video/VIDEOS/NN <name>/`, never mixed.
- Thumbnails bright, warm and colourful, one clear focal subject, type large
  enough to read at 320 px. No small subtitle. Offer 2-3 options.

## Format, as of 2026-10-10

Superseding the old "3-5 minutes" rule:

- **Shorts about 36 s**, nine shots of 4 s, real-speed playback. Not 54 s: his
  own numbers say every Short on the channel is 10-13 s except one at 33 s that
  held 94%, so 33 s is proven and beyond that is a guess. Do not argue for
  longer Shorts on watch-time grounds (see [[project-youtube-ambient]]).
- **Long-form 1-3 hours**, never 20 minutes. Twenty minutes is too long to hold
  a Shorts audience and too short for anyone searching for sleep music.
- **No channel branding burned into the picture.** He rejected a small
  `FIRESIDE / 01` series label outright. No subscribe captions either.
- **Quotes and thoughts on Shorts: yes, but fast.** Six single lines, each on
  screen about 3.8 s with 0.4 s fades, one arriving every 6 s. He twice said
  text that lingers makes the video feel slow. Write original lines, not famous
  quotes: most English translations of Marcus Aurelius, Rumi and Lao Tzu are
  still in copyright.
- White text needs a **4 px dark outline**, not just a drop shadow. A shadow
  disappears against flame or white fog.

**Why:** he judges pace and legibility by watching, and has corrected both
several times. Each correction was right.

**How to apply:** render one video first, pull frames from every text window
and every bright shot, and look at them before building the rest of a batch.
Several faults this session (a mislabelled clip showing a dog in a fireplace
video, unreadable text on fog, duplicate footage inside one video) were
invisible in the config and only showed up in the rendered file. Do not report
a batch as ready without having looked at frames from it.
