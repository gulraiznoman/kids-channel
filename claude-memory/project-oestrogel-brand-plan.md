Building the **Oestrogel 2027 brand plan** for OBS Pharma (Pvt) Ltd, Pakistan. Oestrogel is
Besins Healthcare's transdermal 17β-estradiol gel (80 g tube, 0.06%), re-launched in Pakistan
August 2025. User works in Product Management, Women's Health Care.

**Decisions already settled — do not re-litigate:**

- **Competitors are hormonal only**, plus non-hormonal fezolinetant. Direct orals: **Climen,
  Estranor, Tibolone**. Second set: **Fezlon, Fezolit**. Calcium, Evion, Surbex, soya
  isoflavones are NOT competitors even though they appear in menopause prescriptions.
- **Progyluton and Progynova are NOT real competitors.** Large in value and the main driver of
  category growth, but only ~2,651 menopause Rx each (panel floor) — their volume is earned in
  PCOS, cycle regulation and endometrial preparation. Their units are also declining
  (−24.5% and −5.1%) while value rises on price.
- **Dose language:** always "lowest effective dose 1.25 g = 0.75 mg" and "recommended dose
  2.5 g = 1.5 mg". Never "1 pump / 2 pumps".
- **Positioning:** *"Estrogen deficiency? Think Oestrogel."* — the safer, body-identical
  estrogen deficiency solution. An earlier Urdu campaign idea ("jo hamara khayal rakhti hai")
  was dropped.
- **Never show the 35–40% YTD achievement figure.** Management deck leads with green flags,
  then real challenges, then what is being done, then 2027. Framing must never invite
  "why aren't you already in that segment" — say the sequencing was deliberate.
- No slide numbers, no footer bar. Source references in a small footer line only.

**Key numbers (verify before reuse — these age):**
- Offtake Oct-2025 to Aug-2026: 5,791 packs, PKR 12.3 m, +54% on month one
- Share of estradiol segment: 9.6% value / 4.3% unit
- Category (menopause-relevant basket) PKR 906 m, +29.7% CAGR; 95.5% still oral
- Trade PKR 2,125, retail PKR 2,500. Retail = trade ÷ 0.85
- Cost/day: Oestrogel 39 (low dose) / 78 (rec dose) vs fezolinetant 100–120
- 2027 target 20,000 packs = PKR 42.5 m at trade
