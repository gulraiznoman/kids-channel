User writes Inflectiv (inflectiv.ai) social posts and blogs. Plans are xlsx in Downloads, e.g. "Inflectiv_Sept-Oct_Plan_2026_v3.xlsx" (latest as of 2026-09-11); blogs are .docx there too.

Sheet columns: Caption, Short Caption (non-thread platforms), Graphic Heading, Graphic With Narwhal, Graphic Without Narwhal. Every post gets both graphic versions. Mascot: blue narwhal, sunglasses, white tee, orange shorts. Layout: logo top-left, heading on stacked blue/orange bars at left, art at right.

New narrative since the site relaunch (~2026-09-07): "Every dataset is a dead file. Inflectiv's are alive." You buy questions, not files: 1 credit per question in USDC, answers cite dated, confidence-scored records, a miss is free, annotators pay half, agents write back so datasets grow. Site stats: 30k+ users, 30k+ datasets, ~7k agents, 5 MCP clients, 2,841,096 records. The 10K/9K figures in older plans are stale.

Jaka Kotnik reviews content. His 2026-09-09 feedback: talk about what Inflectiv is and the new narrative, never about page layout or "we redesigned the site".

Blog URLs follow https://inflectiv.ai/blog/<slug>.

**Why:** future sessions can pick up plan files, format and positioning without re-deriving them.
**How to apply:** match the sheet columns, use site numbers, frame around the narrative. See [[feedback-inflectiv-copy]] and [[feedback-versioned-deliverables]].
