# Gul & Mishu content log (check BEFORE making any new video)

Rule: a new video must not repeat an existing one. Before building, compare it with this log:
- **Topic + format**: never the same topic in the same format twice (e.g. a second "balloon colour names" video).
- **Items**: reusing a drawing is fine, but no more than about 1/3 of a new video's items may already have been
  taught the same way in another video. Reused items must be shown in a NEW way (new question, game, story, song).
- **Script**: no copied lines or scenes from another video. Fresh intro, fresh fun lines, fresh ending.
- **Look**: change at least two of: background/setting, outfits, music key/tempo/instruments, section types, length.
- **Shorts**: cut from a long video only when the Short adds a new hook; never post the same clip twice.
Add every new video to this log the day it is made.
